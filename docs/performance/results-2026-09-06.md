# Retained performance results

The goal is to make a busy Hype Comms workspace feel as fast as a small one: show useful content promptly, keep typing and reading responsive, and catch up without work proportional to every cached conversation. The initial budgets are in the [baseline](baseline-2026-09-06.md). The work improves several measured cases; it does not establish that the full goal is met.

These are measurements on one macOS arm64 machine, using production-built but unpackaged Electron, hidden windows, synthetic messages and local PostgreSQL. Each comparison below has its own fixture and probe version. Do not combine them into one matched experiment or treat small publication smoke runs as performance estimates.

## What remains open

Long-history first visits still reach roughly 104–117 ms in later cycles, beyond the initial 100 ms budget. Some startup repeats were slower, including a later search candidate run. Search depends on PostgreSQL worker availability: repository p95 was about 206 ms with four concurrent requests, 398 ms with eight, and 132 ms for one request with parallel workers disabled. These measurements do not establish performance under production load.

The foreground-window pilots failed the native-focus guard before producing valid samples. Visible performance and normal visible read tracking remain unverified. Hidden-window DOM and animation-frame measurements do not measure physical display latency. Packaged-release, other-platform and broader attachment/thread/task-heavy evidence remain follow-up work.

## Experiments removed

Two virtual-timeline attempts failed reading-position/search checks or increased first-visit latency. Offscreen layout broke tail following and middle-message navigation. A keyed timeline subtree, omitted empty reaction containers, Unreads memoization, parallel crypto batching and several SQL shapes did not establish useful gains. Their application changes and temporary dependencies were removed. Their reports and diagnostic results remain only in the evidence archive so a later attempt can inspect why they were rejected.
