## Highlights

- The divider between conversations and members in the sidebar is resizable. Drag it, use the
  arrow keys, or double-click to restore the previous height. The height is remembered on
  this device.
- Hermes replies in channels now land in the thread under the message they answer. Direct-message
  replies stay flat. Operators can opt in to waking Hermes on follow-ups in threads it has already
  joined.

## Fixes

- Switching conversations puts the keyboard in the message composer, so you can type without an
  extra click. Deep-linked thread replies focus the thread composer.
