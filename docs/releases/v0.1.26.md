## Highlights

- Preferences now includes a Theme designer. Choose a System, Light, or Dark foundation, pick a
  preset or custom accent color, preview the result in a miniature workspace, and apply it with
  Save & apply. The look stays on this device and follows the operating system when System is
  selected.
- Signed macOS builds can show native notifications. Turn them on under Preferences →
  Notifications; macOS will ask for permission once. Notifications cover direct messages,
  mentions, and replies in threads you participate in. They show the author and conversation, not
  the message body, unless you also enable preview. Clicking a notification restores Hype Comms
  and opens that conversation.

## Known limitations

- Native notifications are a macOS-only opt-in. They stay off until you enable them. Windows and
  Linux builds still do not include native notification presentation.
- If you deny the macOS permission prompt, turn Hype Comms back on in System Settings, then use
  Refresh capability in Preferences.
- Theme designs are stored on this device only and do not sync across machines.
