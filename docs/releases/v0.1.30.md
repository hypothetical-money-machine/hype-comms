## Highlights

- Channels and DMs can attach files. Use Attach in the composer, including in thread replies, and
  send with a caption or as a file-only message (the filename becomes the body). Shared files show
  as chips on the message; Open launches them. Each conversation also has a Files pane with name,
  uploader, size, date, Open, and Show in chat.
- Catch up in the sidebar now includes Unreads. Mentions appear first, other unread conversations
  follow, and clicking a row opens that conversation. Counts match the existing sidebar badges.
  Opening the Unreads list does not mark anything read.
- Compact mode now tightens message, sidebar, and chrome spacing so more rows fit on screen.
  Comfortable density stays the default. The same Preferences checkbox and shortcut still hide the
  rail and sidebar until you reveal them.
- Preferences → Messages includes an optional fenced-blockquote setting. Choose `"""` or `>>>` to
  treat matching lines as a multiline quote. Ordinary Markdown quoting stays the default. Changing
  the setting re-renders existing messages without rewriting stored text.

## Known limitations

- Attachments are limited to 25 MiB and 10 files per message. Executables and similar installers
  are rejected.
- Desktop clients before 0.1.30 do not show attachments even when the workspace server has them.
- Fenced-blockquote and compact-mode preferences are stored on this device and do not sync.
